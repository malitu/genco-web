## What we do

We coordinate import and export operations, based in Bornova, Izmir. We are
not a consultancy that only delivers reports; we work as an operating partner
for clients across global trade networks.

Slogan: *"Take your business around the world — we'll be your compass."*

## Sectors

1. **Engineering steel** — steel bar import/export, EN and ASTM compliance,
   dimensional and tolerance management
2. **Yachting & marine equipment** — accessories and safety equipment, CE and
   registration compliance
3. **Seed trade** — seed import/export, product permits, analysis processes,
   plant health and certification follow-up
4. **Medical & healthcare** — medical devices and surgical consumables,
   supplier and buyer coordination by regulatory requirement
5. **Packaging** — design, pre-press support and printing, from idea to
   printed product
6. **Femtech & health technology** — women's health product exports, MDR
   scope and technical file preparation

## Services

- **Outsourced export department** — an international sales team without
  building one in-house
- **Qualified sourcing and manufacturer audits** — finding the right
  manufacturer, capacity and quality audits
- **Tolerance and standards compliance** — EN, ASTM and HS code compliance
  secured with a technical file
- **Data-driven buyer and distributor finding** — commercial outreach that
  reaches decision-makers directly
- **Turnkey import** — coordination from pre-research through order tracking
- **Packaging design, pre-press support and printing**

## How we work

Research → Connect → Execute → Grow. We do not leave foreign trade on paper:
we run the operation on the factory floor, at the port and during shipment.
Technical compliance is verified in the field, not only at the desk.
